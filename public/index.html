How it works

  1. 1

    Find. SAM 3.1 finds the sheet of A4, the window and the buyer's hands in the clip.

  2. 2

    Measure. Plain code works out millimetres from the sheet's known 210 by 297 mm size, then picks a pole and the cut.

  3. 3

    Reply. Muse Voice Transcribe hears what the buyer asked. Muse Spark drafts my reply.

  4. 4

    Show. Muse Image paints the pole on their wall, and SAM cuts it out so nothing else in the photo changes.

Every number in the reply comes from the measuring code. The reply is checked and rejected if it contains any number the code didn't produce.
